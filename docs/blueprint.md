# **App Name**: FixMyMobile

## Core Features:

- Homepage Showcase: Display a prominent logo, an eye-catching banner, featured products, a 'Book Repair' call-to-action, and direct contact options (phone, WhatsApp).
- E-commerce Shop: Implement a shop with product browsing by categories, detailed product pages, 'Add to Cart' functionality, and a secure checkout process with Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI payment options.
- Product Pages: Create detailed pages for each product, including high-quality images, price, description, and related product suggestions to encourage further purchases.
- Repair Appointment System: Develop a repair appointment form where users can specify their device, the problem encountered, and their preferred date and time for the repair. Send a confirmation message after submission.
- Technician Expertise Showcase: Detail the company's experience, the skills of their technicians, and the use of genuine parts in the 'About Us' section to build trust with customers.
- Interactive Contact Section: Include an interactive map, direct phone and WhatsApp contact options, and a contact form for inquiries, making it easy for customers to reach out.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#293B5F) to inspire confidence and professionalism.
- Background color: Light blue (#DDE6ED), providing a calm, trustworthy atmosphere.
- Accent color: Light orange (#F0A04B), is used in calls to action for contrast and clear affordance.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a versatile sans-serif font known for its legibility and modern aesthetic, suitable for headlines and body text.
- Use clean, minimalist icons to represent product categories, repair types, and contact options for easy navigation.
- Maintain a grid-based layout for consistent spacing and alignment. Use white space to create visual breathing room and direct attention to key elements.
- Incorporate subtle transitions and animations for interactive elements like the 'Add to Cart' button or when submitting the repair appointment form to give feedback.
